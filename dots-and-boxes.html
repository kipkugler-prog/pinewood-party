    How to play
    • Take turns joining two neighboring dots with a line, across or down, never diagonal.
    • Draw the fourth side of a box to claim it. Your initial goes inside and you go again.
    • When every box is claimed, whoever has the most boxes wins.
    • Tap anywhere close to a gap between two dots to draw there.
    • Careful with the third side of a box: it hands your opponent a free one.
